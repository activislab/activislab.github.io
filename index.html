research programme

vision is an active process.

our work asks how the visual system selects, predicts and stabilises information around each eye movement.

01

selection

presaccadic attention

how visual sensitivity and neural representations change at the future point of fixation before the eyes begin to move.

view current work →
02

prediction

expectation and perception

how prior knowledge about when and what we are likely to see changes perceptual sensitivity and oculomotor decisions.

read the research question →
03

dynamics

neural mechanisms of active vision

how neuronal populations across the visual and oculomotor systems coordinate perception, attention and action.

explore our methods →

featured question

do expectations shape perception and action in the same way?

by manipulating the probability of visual features during saccade preparation, we can test whether predictive information is expressed similarly in perceptual sensitivity and eye-movement initiation.

working principle

perception and action may draw on distinct forms of predictive visual processing—even when they are directed toward the same object.
